# Line Balancing, SAM Values and Capacity Policy

## Standard allowed minutes by style

Capacity is derived from the standard allowed minutes (SAM) of each style and
the number of operators on the line. A line of 30 operators working an eight
hour shift at 85 percent efficiency yields 12,240 productive minutes.

| SKU | Style | SAM | Realistic daily output per 30 operators |
|---|---|---|---|
| GAR-001 | Classic Black Polo | 12.5 | 980 units |
| GAR-002 | White Cotton T-Shirt | 8.0 | 1,530 units |
| GAR-003 | Navy Formal Shirt | 22.0 | 555 units |
| GAR-004 | Grey Hoodie | 26.0 | 470 units |
| GAR-005 | Women's Casual Top | 14.0 | 875 units |
| GAR-006 | Blue Denim Shirt | 24.5 | 500 units |
| GAR-007 | Green Sports T-Shirt | 9.5 | 1,290 units |
| GAR-008 | Beige Cargo Pants | 28.0 | 437 units |

## The 85 percent utilisation rule

A line running at or above 85 percent sustained utilisation is treated as a
bottleneck. This is not an arbitrary limit. Above 85 percent there is no slack
to absorb absenteeism, machine breakdown or rework, so any disturbance
propagates directly into the delivery date.

A bottleneck line must not be given additional volume without either a
reallocation to another line or approved overtime. Planning a line above 95
percent is refused outright: the schedule will not survive contact with a
single sick operator.

## Efficiency ramp for new styles

A style running for the first time does not reach its target efficiency
immediately. Plan the ramp as follows: day one at 60 percent, day two at 75
percent, day three at 85 percent, and full efficiency from day four.

For a short order of under 2,000 units the entire run may sit inside the ramp,
so use 75 percent rather than 85 percent when quoting the date.

## Overtime policy

Overtime is capped at two hours per operator per day and a maximum of three
consecutive days. Beyond that, quality data shows the defect rate rising faster
than the added output, which produces rework that costs more capacity than the
overtime bought.

Overtime requires production manager approval and is never applied
automatically by the planning system.
