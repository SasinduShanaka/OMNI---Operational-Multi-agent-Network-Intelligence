# Line Changeover Between Product Categories

## Why changeover time is protected capacity

A line switching from one product category to another loses productive hours to
machine reconfiguration, needle and thread changes, attachment swaps and a
re-balanced operator layout. This lost time is not idle time and must be
subtracted from available capacity before a feasibility answer is given.

## Standard changeover durations

| From | To | Duration | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Knit to knit (polo to t-shirt) | | 3 hours | Needle and thread change only |
| Knit to woven (polo to formal shirt) | | 1.5 shifts | Full attachment and folder change |
| Woven to knit | | 1 shift | Attachments removed, overlock reset |
| Any category to fleece or hoodie | | 1 shift | Heavier needles, feed adjustment |
| Denim to any lighter fabric | | 1.5 shifts | Deep clean required for indigo migration |

One shift is eight productive hours. LINE-001 and LINE-002 run knits, LINE-003
runs woven formal shirts and denim, and LINE-004 runs fleece and heavier
constructions. Moving a style off its home line always incurs a changeover.

## Changeover and reallocation decisions

When a shortfall is covered by moving volume to another line, the changeover
cost is charged to the receiving line, not the originating one. A reallocation
that saves two days of capacity but costs 1.5 shifts of changeover is only
worthwhile above roughly 1,500 units.

Below that threshold, overtime on the home line is the cheaper option and
should be proposed instead.

## Approval

Any changeover longer than one shift requires production manager approval
because it consumes capacity already committed to another order. The agent may
propose the move but must not treat the capacity as released until the approval
is recorded.
