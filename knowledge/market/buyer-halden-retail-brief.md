# Halden Retail — Formal and Denim Programme Brief

## Account overview

Halden Retail buys GAR-003 Navy Formal Shirt and GAR-006 Blue Denim Shirt for a
mid-market European high street chain. Volumes are smaller than Northwind but
the margin is higher and the quality expectation is stricter.

## Demand pattern

Formal shirt demand is counter-seasonal to the knit business. It peaks between
August and October for the back-to-work and autumn season, and again in a
smaller December window for gifting. The quietest months are consistently May
through July, where monthly offtake can fall to half the annual average.

A declining trend observed in a May or June forecast for GAR-003 is therefore a
seasonal trough and not a structural decline. Reading it as a decline and
cutting the fabric order has caused a stockout in two of the last three years.

## Denim programme

GAR-006 was introduced as a trial in 2025 and Halden has confirmed it moves to
a full programme in 2026 with an expected 40 percent volume increase over the
trial year. Growth in denim forecasts should therefore be expected to continue
rather than flatten.

Halden requires a wash standard approval on every denim bulk, which adds a week
between fabric arrival and cutting.

## Quality and compliance requirements

Halden audits the factory twice a year and requires OEKO-TEX certification on
all trims used in their programmes. Any supplier substitution on Halden styles
requires written approval and an updated certificate before bulk cutting.
