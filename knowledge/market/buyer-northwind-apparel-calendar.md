# Northwind Apparel — Order Calendar and Programme Notes

## Account overview

Northwind Apparel is the largest account for basic knits and takes the majority
of GAR-001 Classic Black Polo and GAR-002 White Cotton T-Shirt volume. The
account works on a replenishment model rather than seasonal drops, so demand is
steadier than the fashion accounts but responds sharply to their promotions.

## Order windows

Northwind places four indicative buys a year and confirms quantities six weeks
before each ship window.

| Window | Placed | Ships | Typical volume |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spring replenishment | January | March | 8,000 to 10,000 units |
| Mid-year promotion | April | June | 12,000 to 15,000 units |
| Autumn replenishment | July | September | 9,000 to 11,000 units |
| Holiday programme | September | November | 14,000 to 18,000 units |

The mid-year and holiday windows are consistently the two largest. A forecast
that projects a flat line through June or November is understating demand,
because those uplifts are programme-driven rather than random variation.

## Promotion behaviour

Northwind runs a two-for-one polo promotion in their June and November
campaigns. Historically this lifts GAR-001 by 25 to 35 percent above the
preceding quarter's monthly average, and lifts GAR-002 by roughly 15 percent
as an attachment purchase.

The promotion is confirmed to us about eight weeks ahead. Where a forecast
increase coincides with a known promotion window, the increase should be
treated as expected rather than as a new trend, because volume returns to the
baseline in the following month.

## Service expectations

Northwind allows a three day delivery window either side of the confirmed date
and applies a 2 percent invoice deduction beyond that. They do not accept
partial shipments on replenishment orders.
