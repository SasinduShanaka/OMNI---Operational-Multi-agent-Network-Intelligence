# Meridian Sportswear — Growth Programme Notes

## Account overview

Meridian Sportswear is the newest account and buys GAR-007 Green Sports T-Shirt
along with a growing share of GAR-005 Women's Casual Top. The account was
onboarded in mid-2025 and is still scaling, so its history is short and the
trend in it is real growth rather than seasonal noise.

## Growth trajectory

Meridian has committed to a phased volume increase through 2026: roughly 3,000
units a month in the first quarter, 4,500 in the second, and an indicated 6,000
a month in the second half subject to sell-through.

A forecast built on the 2025 history alone will understate this account, because
the history predates the committed step-up. Where a GAR-007 forecast looks flat
against a rising order book, the order book is the better signal.

## Product direction

Meridian is moving its range towards recycled polyester and has asked for a
recycled content option on GAR-007 from the third quarter. This will require a
new fabric approval and a supplier with the relevant certification, and it adds
roughly three weeks to the first bulk.

## Risk notes

The account is concentrated in a single online retailer. A change in that
retailer's promotional calendar moves Meridian's volume sharply in either
direction with little notice, so forecasts for GAR-007 should be reviewed
monthly rather than quarterly, and the buffer stock target should be held at
the higher end of the normal range.
